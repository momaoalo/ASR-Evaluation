How the averages are calculated

Average WER / CER: arithmetic mean of the per-case rates. Each shared case has equal weight. Corpus WER / CER: total word / character edits divided by total reference words / characters. Corpus rates drive the charts. S / D / I: word substitutions, deletions and insertions on exactly those same shared cases.

Latest comparable observations only; repeated attempts are not counted twice in a model/configuration. Failed outputs are not zero scores. Cleaning / No cleaning updates all these values together.
